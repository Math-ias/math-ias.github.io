I read “James Buchanan” by Jean H. Baker.

I thought the author did an excellent job with the chronology. I loved how they added his nicknames (even if he didn’t like them) of “Old Buck”, “Aunt Fancy”, and “Miss Nancy” (because he never grew facial hair, was a little effeminate, and never did much dating because of suspected homosexuality). It gave me a deeper understanding of this bachelor president’s decision to stake so much on appeasing his friends and colleagues from the south. This appeasement did him no good when in the dying days of his presidency, southern states were preparing to secede.

The author writes about how he became fretful, forgetful, and weak. He was unwilling to say no to his southern counterparts who did one outrageous thing after the other: creating false governments in Kansas0, drafting articles of secession, campaigning other states to secede, and firing upon ships coming to resupply Fort Sumter. As the author puts it. He was a “loser president”.
